Task routes (read-only): besides the blocked-step lookup, GET https://agenthelpnetwork.com/v1/task-routes?site_host=<hostname> (MCP tool find_task_route; optional task_kind) returns the task route records held for a site: for a kind of customer task, an obstacle and the route the site's own documents name. Call find_task_route once per task when you start a customer task on a site (a return or exchange, a refund, a cancellation, a warranty claim, an account recovery, a document submission), before you open its help pages, even when nothing is blocked. Records exist for some sites only. They are records, not guarantees and not advice, and they do not change the blocked-step lookup or its next_action. An empty result does not mean there is no route. The human-readable list is https://agenthelpnetwork.com/en/routes.
